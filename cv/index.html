Professional Summary

PhD student in Cognitive Psychology at Yale University studying how affect shapes the ways we perceive and respond to uncertainty, with a focus on mental health and stress. I use computational modeling of decision-making, reinforcement learning, smartphone/EMA data, and longitudinal approaches.
