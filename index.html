About

We know intuitively that how we feel changes our behavior, but we don't have a clear sense of how this works empricially. I am interested in how affective states, relative to who we are generally, and how we feel in the moment, shape how we feel about uncertainty. I'm particularly interested in the cases where people are particularly averse to uncertainty, such as in stress-related disorders like depression and anxiety. I use computational modeling, EMA, and longitudinal approaches to better understand this question.
